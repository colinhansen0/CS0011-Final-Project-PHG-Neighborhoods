# CS0011-Final-Project-PHG-Neighborhoods
Final group project for course CS0011 where we analyze various datasets regarding neighborhoods around the city of Pittsburgh PA to come to a conclusion on which neighborhood is best for our metric of choice.

### Drinking Fountains Contribution
- **Team Member:** Rahul Basnet — `rahulbasnet634@gmail.com` (`rahul_notebook.ipynb`)
- **Dataset Used:** [City of Pittsburgh Water Features (`data.csv`)](https://data.wprdc.org/dataset/fe7cfb11-9f33-4590-a5ee-04419f3f974a/resource/513290a6-2bac-4e41-8029-354cbda6a7b7/download/data.csv) — Water fountains, spray fountains and other assets which provide water for public use. ### Walkability Contribution
- **Team Member:** Colin Hansen — `colinhansen0@gmail.com` (`colin/colin_notebook.ipynb`)
- **Datasets Used:**
  - [Sidewalk to Street "Walkability" Ratio (`colin/sidewalkstreetratioupload.csv`)](https://data.wprdc.org/dataset/sidewalk-to-street-walkability-ratio) — Total sidewalk and street length for every census block group in Allegheny County (2021), with trails, crosswalks, highways, and alleys excluded. Used to calculate a sidewalk-to-street ratio for each Pittsburgh neighborhood, where a higher ratio means more walkable.
  - [2010 Census Block Groups (`colin/2010NeighborhoodBlocks.csv`)](https://data.wprdc.org/dataset/2010-census-block-groups) — City of Pittsburgh list of every census block group and the neighborhood it belongs to. Used to match the walkability data to all 90 Pittsburgh neighborhoods.
